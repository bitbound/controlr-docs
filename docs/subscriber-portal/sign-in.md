## Signing In

The portal has its own accounts. There is no separate registration. You sign in with GitHub, and the portal creates your account from your GitHub identity the first time.

1. Open [https://portal.controlr.app](https://portal.controlr.app).
2. Select **Sign in with GitHub**. If you are already signed in, the landing page shows **Open dashboard** instead.
3. GitHub asks you to authorize the portal. Approve the request.

The portal sends you back to the dashboard after GitHub approves the sign-in.

## What The Portal Reads From GitHub

On your first sign-in the portal shows a dialog titled **How your GitHub permissions are used**. It explains what the portal reads from your GitHub account and why. Select **Got it** to continue. You cannot dismiss the dialog by clicking outside it.

The portal reads your profile, your email, your organization memberships, and your sponsorship status. It uses these to identify you, match you to your sponsorship, and discover any sponsoring organizations you belong to.

For build notifications to reach you, your GitHub account needs an email address. The permissions dialog tells you if your account has none. Add one in your GitHub profile settings if it is missing.

## Personal And Organization Contexts

Your GitHub login is how you sign in. The account context is the sponsor whose benefits you are viewing. They are kept separate.

- In a personal context, you manage your own sponsor account.
- In an organization context, you manage a sponsoring organization's account.

You can only manage an organization you have access to. Organization admins get access automatically. Other members need to be granted access. See [Organization Access](/docs/subscriber-portal/organization-access) for how access is granted and how to switch contexts.

## If Your GitHub Access Expires

The portal checks your sponsorship and organization membership against GitHub over time. If your stored authorization stops working, the dashboard shows a warning that sponsorship and organization checks are paused. Select **Sign in with GitHub** again to restore them.
